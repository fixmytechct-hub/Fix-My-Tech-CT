Who I Am

A Local Tech You Can Actually Reach

Fix My Tech CT is a small local IT shop serving Fairfield County, CT and Westchester, NY. When you call, you do not get a call center. You get me. No phone trees, no ticket numbers, no "your call is important to us."

I am CompTIA A+ certified and work in English and Spanish. Every job starts with an upfront flat-rate quote that you approve before any work begins, and if we cannot fix it, you do not pay.

My favorite part of the job is the teaching. After every repair I walk you through what happened and show you a few things, so it does not happen again and you are never left in the dark.
