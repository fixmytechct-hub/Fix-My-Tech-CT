FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about pricing, repairs, data recovery and how it all works. Still have a question? Call or text and Dan answers.

📞 Call (203) 816-0678 Send a Message
How much does virus removal cost?
Virus and malware removal is a flat $85. You get the price before any work starts and you approve it first.
What if you cannot fix my problem?
If we cannot fix it, you do not pay. Every repair starts with an upfront flat-rate quote that you approve before work begins.
Do you come to me, or can you fix things remotely?
Both. I work on-site across Fairfield County, CT and Westchester, NY, and remote support is available anywhere. You choose what is easier.
How do I get a price?
Call or text (203) 816-0678 or use the contact form. Tell me what is going on and I will give you a flat-rate quote before any work begins.
Can you recover photos or files from a dead phone, memory card or hard drive?
Often yes, but it depends on the damage. I look at the device, quote the price after that, and tell you what is realistic before you decide. If the device is not working right, stop using it and do not keep trying to power it on, since that can make recovery harder.
Do you build custom PCs?
Yes. I pick parts for your budget and how you use the computer, then build it, manage the cables, test it and set it up for you. Pricing is a custom quote based on the parts.
Can you clean or repair my graphics card?
I can clean out dust, check the fans, refresh the thermal paste and test temperatures. This is a good fit for a GPU that runs hot or loud. I will quote it first.
What areas do you serve?
On-site in Stamford, Greenwich, Norwalk, Westport, Fairfield, Darien, New Canaan and Bridgeport in Connecticut, and White Plains, Scarsdale, Yonkers and New Rochelle in New York. Remote help is available anywhere.
Do you speak Spanish?
Yes. I work in English and Spanish, so you can get tech support in the language you are most comfortable with.
What are your hours and how fast do you respond?
I am available Mon–Sat, 9am–7pm. I respond the same day, usually within the hour. Call or text (203) 816-0678.
Can you help with my printer?
Yes. Printer setup and troubleshooting is $60. I install drivers, connect it wirelessly, test it from all your devices and show you how to reconnect.
Do you offer monthly support plans?
Yes. Plans start at $149 per month and include remote support and a monthly check-in. See the plans on the services page.
